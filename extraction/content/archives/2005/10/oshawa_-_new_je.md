**Day 2**

2 countries in two days; At this rate, if I travel for a year we will have visited 365 countries. We're in New Jersey visiting Jon's family at the moment and should be heading into Brooklyn later this evening.

Apparently there is some drama with the NYC Subways, CNN knows a lot more than I do: [New York spends day on alert after false alarm](http://www.cnn.com/2005/US/10/07/newyork.subways/index.html).

Also, I just found out that The Sudbury Star has a front page story about our trip. I haven't seen it for myself but, if the rumors are true than it could mean a boost to our readership. A quick search online indicates that they have an average daily [readership](http://www.micromedia.ca/Products_Services/CdnNewsstandDailies_0404.htm) of 19,000. These are exciting times for me.
