[![All You Need For A Trip Around The World](http://static.flickr.com/28/49558376_ba586c4e95_t.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/49558376/ "Photo Sharing")  
  
Success. It seemed like an impossible task but I did it. I packed my entire life into these two small bags. Just two bags of clothing and gear. I'm pretty sure I have roughly the same volume of stuff to go around the world as many people bring to a cottage for a long weekend. Except for a few small items that still need to be picked up in Toronto, beginning tomorrow, I'll be living exclusively from these two bags.

Of course my luggage didn't always look as neat and tidy as you see pictured above. I've developed a highly effective, two-step packing strategy that I might consider trademarking and selling if I didn't already have my hands full with this trip. Consider yourselves lucky, you get this packing advice free of charge. Here it is: [![Gadgets and Stuff - To Bring Around the World](http://static.flickr.com/29/49540177_1a2d593723_t.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/49540177/ "Photo Sharing")

1. Throw everything that you think you might want to use while travelling onto a pile on the floor. Don't eliminate anything. If you think you can use it, add it to the pile. All the important decisions come later, in step two.

2. Begin removing things from the pile while chanting to yourself[![Clothes to Bring Around the World](http://static.flickr.com/26/49535727_b44aa0d7db_t.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/49535727/ "Photo Sharing")  
  
  
 my new favorite mantra: "Less is more." As soon as the pile has diminished enough to fit into the luggage you've got, pack it up and then take a nap.

That's it. It's that easy. In my case it worked out to two easily carried bags. A 50 Litre backpack and a 58 Litre [MEC](http://www.mountainco-op.com/Products/product_detail.jsp;jsessionid=DDjFf5QKGfxhLKx0QFGWtsXJLhY5XKYpMV0xLQwd7s1FpwmFCfwv!1945887133?PRODUCT<>prd_id=845524441772735&FOLDER<>folder_id=2534374302699795&bmUID=1128489733975) duffle bag. (My daypack is actually inside the duffle bag.)

Tomorrow will be a trial run for me as I'll be making my way over to Sudbury (2hrs) to board a bus down to Toronto (5.5hrs), which is officially where we want to leave from, and return to.

I plan to provide a breakdown of what I'm bringing and what I've decided to leave behind in the [FAQ](http://www.theroadislife.com/faq.htm) section very soon.
