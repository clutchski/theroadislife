**Day 6**

[![Surfing at Bryant Park](http://static.flickr.com/32/51679141_2334822c82_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/51679141/ "Photo Sharing")  
  
[Bryant Park](http://www.bryantpark.org/); Come for the free Wi-Fi, stay for the urban oasis. As long as it's not raining, which is hardly ever, I'm planning to spend some time here each day, on their free wireless internet network updating this site and generally getting my daily fix.

Google has recently put in a bid to provide free [wi-fi to the entire city of San Francisco](http://www.engadget.com/entry/1234000783061372/), and I'd been reading that they'd been experimenting with [wi-fi in New York City](http://evans.blogware.com/blog/_archives/2005/9/27/1263192.html). I'm guessing Bryant Park is their Area 51. Though it doesn't seem to me to be much of a secret as they've got their name on every brochure and sign posted in the park. In any case, if it's free, I'll take it.

Yesterday I mentioned that I was planning to do a little walking on my own to see the city. When I said a 'little' I'd envisioned a few hours at the most. What I didn't imagine was that I'd walk from 103rd street all the way down to [Battery Park](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_Park_%28New_York%29), then back up to Times Square (42nd st.) I'm certainly no expert in NYC geography but I'm pretty sure this works out to over 200 city blocks.

Uh oh. Here comes the rain. The trouble with free outdoor wi-fi is that computers don't work well in the rain. I have to go. I apologize if this entry is full of typos and gramatical errors... let it rain, let it rain, let it rain.
