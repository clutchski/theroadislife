From Rio Dulce we hired a boat to take us down the river and into the Carribbean town of Livingston where the pace of living slows to a crawl. I spent most of my time cultivating my love for food. Pictured is a [Garifuna](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garifuna) dish that combines fish, shrimp, crab, banana and coconut milk. mmmmm... tapado...
