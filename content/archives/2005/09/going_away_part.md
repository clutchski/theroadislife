Even though we still have another 20 days or so before we leave, Saturday September 10th was my official [going away party](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/sets/936823/); I, of course had a blast. The greatest thing about hosting a party is that, aside from a few friends of friends, you know everybody. This party was no different. The turn out was great and I caught up with some old friends I otherwise may not have seen before leaving. [Brian](http://www.theroadislife.com/about.htm) also managed to drive down from Sudbury to make an appearance, dispelling the notion, here in Toronto, that he may not actually exist.

I've uploaded the photos to [my flickr account](http://www.flickr.com/photos/theroadislife/). Any time you click any photo, on this site it should bring you straight to [flickr](http://www.flickr.com/). I find the process of hosting photos there much easier than my own webserver.
