This afternoon I got a call from Ryan telling me that he has reached the island of [Hiva Oa](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiva_Oa) in the [Marquesas](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marquesas) Islands. Rumor has it that the post office has internet, but it has been closed for the last three days. The island is extremely isolated. They haven't cleared customs yet because they can't find the one customs officer. Once some minor engine repairs are completed, they'll be back on the open water. The next stop is Taumotu Islands and then onto Tahiti. Ryan figures he'll have internet access by then and will post for real.
